My name is Hassan Amir. In 2024, my brother experienced a stroke and was subsequently diagnosed with cerebral palsy. This experience changed the way my family and I understood the challenges people with cerebral palsy face, especially when it comes to accessibility, communication, and finding reliable information.

Inspired by my brother’s experience, we developed Kingston CP Connect to support individuals with cerebral palsy in Kingston, Ontario. Our goal is to make accurate information easier to access, help people navigate their community, and provide practical communication tools.

This is an evolving community project. We hope to improve it through input from people with CP and their families.
